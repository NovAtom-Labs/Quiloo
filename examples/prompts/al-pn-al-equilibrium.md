# Equilibrium Al / p-Si / n-Si / Al

Simulate the equilibrium electrical characteristics of an Al / p-Si / n-Si / Al
structure using Sentaurus TCAD at 300 K, with no externally applied voltage or
voltage bias.

The p-Si region is uniformly doped with boron at 1 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³, while the n-Si
region is uniformly doped with phosphorus at 1 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³. Aluminum is used as
the metal contact on both sides with a work function of 4.10 eV.

The simulation should be performed under zero-bias equilibrium conditions, with
both contacts maintained at 0 V. No voltage sweep or external potential is
applied.

Determine:

- Electrostatic potential distribution
- Built-in potential across the p-n junction
- Electric-field distribution
- Electron and hole concentration
- Space-charge distribution
- Conduction-band and valence-band profiles
- Fermi-level position
- Electron and hole current densities
- Carrier mobility
- Recombination rates

Use appropriate semiconductor physics models for:

- Fermi statistics
- Carrier mobility
- Shockley-Read-Hall recombination
- Auger recombination
- Band-gap narrowing
